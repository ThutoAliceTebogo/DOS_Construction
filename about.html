About DOS Construction

Building a stronger Botswana through quality, innovation, and integrity.

Our Story

Founded in 2015, DOS Construction began with a vision to raise the standard of construction in Botswana. What started as a small team of passionate engineers has grown into one of the most respected construction companies in the country.

Over the past decade, we have successfully delivered numerous high-profile projects including major water infrastructure developments, modern residential estates, commercial complexes, and critical road networks that connect communities across Botswana.

Our name "DOS" stands for **Dedication, Opportunity, and Success** — values that continue to drive every project we undertake.

Our Mission

To deliver exceptional construction solutions that exceed client expectations while maintaining the highest standards of safety, quality, sustainability, and professionalism.

Our Vision

To be the leading construction company in Botswana and Southern Africa, known for innovation, reliability, and transformative infrastructure development that improves the lives of communities.

Our Core Values

Integrity
