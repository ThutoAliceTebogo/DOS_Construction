Water Projects

Sustainable water infrastructure solutions for a better future

Expertise in Water Infrastructure

DOS Construction specializes in the design and construction of reliable water systems that serve communities, industries, and agriculture across Botswana.

From large-scale dams and reservoirs to modern water distribution networks, we deliver projects that ensure clean, safe, and sustainable water supply.

Our Water Project Services Include:

  • Construction of Dams and Reservoirs
  • Water Reticulation and Distribution Systems
  • Borehole Drilling and Equipping
  • Irrigation Systems and Canals
  • Wastewater Treatment Plants
  • Pump Stations and Storage Tanks
Why Choose Us for Water Projects?
  • • Compliance with Botswana Water Regulations
  • • Use of modern hydraulic modeling
  • • Environmentally sustainable solutions
  • • Long-term maintenance support
